## 🇪🇸 Español

### 👋 Sobre mí

Soy **Nico Ramdohr**, fundador de [**Intelify**](https://intelify.cl). Hoy construyo [**Intelify Inmo App**](https://app.intelify.cl/demo): el sistema de venta para **corredoras e inmobiliarias en Chile**, del lead a la reserva, en una sola plataforma. Contacta y agenda a cada interesado, evalúa su capacidad de compra en la reunión, cotiza con un botón, ordena los documentos para el banco y le muestra a gerencia qué está pasando de verdad.

Antes de eso diseñé y operé sistemas comerciales con IA y automatización para negocios reales. De ahí viene mi obsesión: que cada pieza sea **medible y confiable** — nada de automatizaciones frágiles ni métricas decorativas. Si no se mide y no aguanta producción, no sirve.

### 🛠️ Qué construyo

- **Sistemas comerciales completos** — del anuncio a la reserva, con trazabilidad de cada cliente y de cada acceso a sus datos.
- **Agentes de IA comerciales** — responden por WhatsApp, califican, agendan y derivan al asesor. Con límites claros: no inventan precios ni dan asesoría financiera.
- **Motores de automatización en n8n** — con logs, reintentos, *fallback* y alerta humana. Robustos por diseño.
- **Arquitecturas de datos en Supabase/Postgres** — multi-tenant, reglas de negocio en la base, máquinas de estado, permisos por rol y *Edge Functions*.
- **Integraciones** — WhatsApp Cloud API, Meta Ads y Lead Ads, Google Calendar, correo transaccional y CRM.

## 🇬🇧 English

### 👋 About me

I'm **Nico Ramdohr**, founder of [**Intelify**](https://intelify.cl). Today I'm building [**Intelify Inmo App**](https://app.intelify.cl/demo): the sales system for **real estate brokerages in Chile**, from lead to reservation, on a single platform. It contacts and schedules every prospect, assesses their buying power during the meeting, quotes with one button, organizes the documents for the bank, and shows management what's really going on.

Before that I designed and ran AI and automation commercial systems for real businesses. That's where my obsession comes from: every piece must be **measurable and reliable** — no fragile automations, no vanity metrics. If it can't be measured and can't survive production, it doesn't ship.

### 🏢 Intelify Inmo App

Six modules on a single engine, sharing the same information end to end:

| Module | What it solves |
|---|---|
| 📲 **Appointment Setter** | A Meta lead gets a WhatsApp message instantly and books a consultation without anyone picking up the phone. |
| 🧑‍💼 **Real Estate Advisor** | Qualifies the client, finds the projects that fit and quotes during the same meeting. |
| 📄 **Client Portal** | Clients upload their documents from their phone, with their own password and consent (Chile's Law 21.719). |
| 🏦 **Bank Analyst** | Every file is reviewed and signed off before it reaches the bank, with a record of who checked what. |
| 📊 **Manager** | Funnel, team performance and demand vs. catalog, with data that loads itself. |
| 🔌 **CRM Connection** | If you already use a CRM, every client update reaches it automatically. |

### 🛠️ What I build

- **Complete commercial systems** — from ad to reservation, with traceability of every client and every access to their data.
- **Commercial AI agents** — answer on WhatsApp, qualify, schedule and hand off to the advisor. With clear limits: they don't invent prices or give financial advice.
- **n8n automation engines** — with logs, retries, *fallback* and human alerting. Robust by design.
- **Supabase/Postgres data architectures** — multi-tenant, business rules in the database, state machines, role-based permissions and *Edge Functions*.
- **Integrations** — WhatsApp Cloud API, Meta Ads and Lead Ads, Google Calendar, transactional email and CRM.
